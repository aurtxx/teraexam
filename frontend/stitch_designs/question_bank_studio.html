A parallel-plate capacitor with circular plates of radius R is being charged by a steady external current source I(t) = I_0. At a distance r from the central axis of symmetry between the plates (where r < R), the generalized Ampère-Maxwell law applies:

∮ B̅ · dl̅ = μ₀ ( Ienc + ε₀ dΦEdt )
Eq. Ref: Ampère-Maxwell Generalized

Neglecting fringing effects at the boundaries of the circular plates, which of the following statements is/are TRUE regarding the induced magnetic induction vector B̅(r, t) and the Poynting flux vector S̅ into the dielectric cylinder?

A
Inside the plates (r ≤ R), the magnitude of the magnetic field scales linearly as B(r) = (μ₀ I_0 r) / (2π R²). check_circle Correct Key (Answered 78%)
B
The direction of the Poynting vector S̅ = (1/μ₀) (E̅ × B̅) is directed radially inward toward the central axis everywhere across the boundary. check_circle Correct Key (Answered 64%)
C
The conduction current density inside the region strictly equals the displacement current density, such that J_c = J_d ≠ 0 everywhere in the vacuum gap. Distractor Key (Chosen by 32%)
D
Total energy flow rate per unit time across the cylindrical perimeter of radius R matches the instantaneous rate of electrostatic energy storage d/dt (½ C V²). check_circle Correct Key (Answered 41%)
Figure P-104.B: Vector Projection High-Res Vector
Experimental Verification Testbed: IIT Bombay RF Core Lab
Correct Keys: A, B, D (Score: +4 / -2)

Step 1: Displacement Current Calculation: Since electric field E = V/d = Q/(ε₀ π R²), the rate of change of flux through radius r is given by Φ_E = E π r² = (Q r²) / (ε₀ R²). Thus displacement current I_d(r) = ε₀ (dΦ_E/dt) = I_0 (r² / R²).

Step 2: Ampere Circuital Law Application: Along the loop of radius r, B (2π r) = μ₀ I_d(r) = μ₀ I_0 (r² / R²) ⇒ B(r) = (μ₀ I_0 r) / (2π R²). This proves option (A) correct.

Step 3: Poynting Vector Convergence: E̅ points downwards (charging), B̅ circles clockwise from above. By right-hand cross product rule E̅ × B̅, energy flows purely inward into the capacitor gap, directly replenishing the stored electrostatic dielectric energy.

The rate constant of the decomposition reaction is determined to be k = X × 10−² min−¹. Determine the numerical value of X. (Given: ln(5) = 1.6094, ln(2) = 0.6931, round off to two decimal places).
